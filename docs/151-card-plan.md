# PrayerCircle Card Collection — 151 Card Master Plan

## Vision
Like Pokémon's original 151. A complete collectible set of Bible-scene trading cards, earned through faithfulness (never random chance).

## Set Structure

### Milestone Cards (26) — DONE
Earned through prayer streaks and fasts.
- **Streak:** 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 21, 25, 30, 50, 75, 100 (11 cards)
- **Fast:** 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 21, 25, 30, 40, 60, 100, 365 (12 cards)
- **Starter:** First Task, Curator, Full Set (3 cards)

### Evolution Lines (12) — IN PROGRESS
Three-stage character arcs. Each stage unlocks at a higher milestone.
- **David:** Shepherd (5-streak) → Giant Slayer (15-streak) → King (30-streak)
- **Peter:** Fisherman (5-fast) → Denier (15-fast) → Restored (30-fast)
- **Paul:** Persecutor (10-streak) → Blinded (20-streak) → Apostle (50-streak)
- **Moses:** Basket (10-fast) → Burning Bush (25-fast) → Red Sea (60-fast)

### Genesis Series (20) — PLANNED
Creation, Adam, Eve, Cain & Abel, Noah, The Ark, Rainbow Covenant, Babel, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph's Coat, Joseph in Egypt, etc.

### Exodus Series (20) — PLANNED
Moses, Burning Bush, 10 Plagues (10 cards), Red Sea, Manna, Water from Rock, Ten Commandments, Golden Calf, Tabernacle, etc.

### Kingdom Series (20) — PLANNED
Saul, David, Solomon, Temple, Queen of Sheba, Elijah, Elisha, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Daniel, Shadrach/Meshach/Abednego, Esther, Nehemiah, etc.

### Gospels Series (25) — PLANNED
Nativity, Shepherds, Wise Men, Baptism, Temptation, Water to Wine, Loaves & Fishes, Walking on Water, Transfiguration, Prodigal Son, Good Samaritan, Triumphal Entry, Last Supper, Gethsemane, Cross, Resurrection, Ascension, etc.

### Church Series (15) — PLANNED
Pentecost, Stephen, Philip, Cornelius, Damascus Road, Paul's Journeys, Priscilla & Aquila, Timothy, James, John, Revelation, etc.

### Legendary Series (13) — PLANNED
Special foil cards. The biggest moments.
- Creation, The Fall, The Flood, The Exodus, David & Goliath, The Nativity, The Baptism, The Transfiguration, The Cross, The Resurrection, Pentecost, The Return, New Jerusalem

## Total: 151

## Unlock Design (for non-milestone cards)
- **Genesis/Exodus/Kingdom/Gospels/Church:** Unlock by completing themed challenges (e.g., "Read Genesis 1" unlocks Creation, "Complete 3 prayer streaks" unlocks David, etc.)
- **Legendary:** Unlock by completing major milestones (e.g., 365-day streak, 100-day fast, all cards in a series)
